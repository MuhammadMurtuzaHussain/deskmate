Three things and she's ready. You can come back any time: menu-bar icon → Setup…

4 · From your phone (optional)

…
Message her from the chat apps you already use. Each one is off until you set it up, and only you can reach her through it: anyone else gets one short "not paired" line (bots) or nothing at all (iMessage, Signal). Anything that sends, deletes, buys or runs something asks you on the phone first. More in docs/CHANNELS.md.

6 · Tool servers (MCP, optional)

…
Servers listed in mcp.json, in the same shape as Claude Desktop's config, give her more tools: your files, GitHub, a database. She uses them with Claude as her brain. What a server answers is read as data, never as instructions, and a tool that can change things asks you first once she has read anything from outside.

7 · Scheduled

…
Things she does on her own: say "every weekday at 9 summarise my Slack" or "every hour check if the build is green". Each run is its own Claude turn; the result is said out loud when you're at the desk, sent to your phone when you're away (if a chat app is on), and always written in her day log. Anything that sends, deletes, buys or runs something waits for your yes.

8 · What she remembers and knows

…
Her notes about you are two plain files, USER.md and MEMORY.md; she reads them every time you talk, and you can edit them. She can also search everything you two said and everything she did. After a long task that worked, she writes down how she did it as a skill, and uses it only after your yes.

9 · Permissions and safety

…
Every tool she has gets a level: allow runs it, ask asks you first every time, never takes it away from her. Reading is allow; sending, deleting, buying, running commands, Claude Code jobs and files leaving the Mac ask. Once she has read a web page, a message or anything else from outside, anything risky asks you whatever is set here. Say "yes, always" to one of her questions and that tool stops asking from there. More in docs/SAFETY.md.
Stop everythingstops what she is doing, plans, scheduled jobs, the heartbeat and chat apps; asks about Claude Code jobs. Say "resume" to turn them back on.
